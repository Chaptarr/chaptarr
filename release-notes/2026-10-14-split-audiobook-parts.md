Audiobooks released in several parts, such as "(1 of 2)" and "(2 of 2)", now import side by side instead of each part replacing the previous one. New {ReleasePart} and {ReleaseParts} naming tokens are available. Parts already imported before this update are not re-identified, so only new imports are protected.

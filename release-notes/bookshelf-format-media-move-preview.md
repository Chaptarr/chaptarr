ChaptarrNG can preview and move an author's registered audiobook or ebook files to a new format-specific location, including registered book sidecars resolved from the linked book's format root. A bulk workflow previews all selected authors together; destination conflicts block the move, paths that escape the author folders are rejected, and untracked files and author-wide extras stay in place.

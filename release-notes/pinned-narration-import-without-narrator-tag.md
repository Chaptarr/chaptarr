Audiobooks requested for a specific narrator, including a second narration of a book you already have, now import when the files carry no narrator tag but their length matches the requested recording and no other narration of the book comes as close. Files that name a different narrator are still rejected. The pinned edition's title also matches without an Audible-style ", Book N" suffix.
